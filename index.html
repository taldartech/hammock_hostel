Why Hammock Hostels?

Hammock Hostels is more than just a place to stay — it’s a social travel community built for backpackers, solo travellers, digital nomads and global explorers looking to connect through meaningful experiences.

Whether you are searching for the best hostel in Jaipur for backpackers, a budget hostel in Mumbai for solo travellers, or an affordable social hostel in India, Hammock combines comfort, community and culture under one roof.

From Food tours, community events and walking tours to coworking-friendly spaces and local experiences, our hostels are designed to help travellers meet people, share stories and create unforgettable memories.

Perfect for:

  • solo travellers
  • backpackers
  • digital nomads
  • international tourists
  • young travellers
  • remote workers

With clean and safe spaces, free wifi, social vibes and prime locations near major tourist attractions, Hammock Hostels offers some of the best budget backpacker accommodation in Jaipur and Mumbai.

Because at Hammock, it was never just about the bed.
It’s about the people you meet along the way.
