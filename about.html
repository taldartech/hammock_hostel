Our story

Hammock Hostels wasn’t born out of a snap decision. It began as a feeling.

Years ago, our co-founder Apurva stayed at a hostel in San Francisco that left a lasting impression on him. It wasn’t just the city or the building — it was the feeling of connection, warmth and belonging the hostel created. It made him feel seen, welcomed and inspired. In that moment, he knew he wanted to build something that could make others feel the same way someday.

Life moved on, careers happened and almost a decade passed before that idea resurfaced again.

That’s when our second co-founder, Priyanka, entered the picture. What started as a brief webinar interaction slowly turned into coffee catchups, long conversations about travel, culture and community, and eventually the buried idea found its way back to life.

Apurva convinced Priyanka to move back to India from Australia and take a leap of faith together. And that leap became Hammock Hostels.

Today, with two hostels and many more to come, our vision remains simple — to create more than just accommodation. We want to build spaces that bring people together through shared experiences, meaningful connections and unforgettable memories.

Yes, we provide clean, safe and comfortable hostels — but to us, that is simply the bare minimum.

What truly matters is the feeling you leave with.

The people you meet, the stories exchanged over chai, the rooftop conversations, the walking tours, the laughter shared with strangers who become friends — these are the moments that define Hammock.

We want every guest to experience the warmth of Indian hospitality, the joy of human connection and the magic of travel in a way they’ll remember long after they leave our doors.
